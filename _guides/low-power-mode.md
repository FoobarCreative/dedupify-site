Extend your battery life by **20-30%** when running low on power with Low Power Mode.

## Why This Matters

Low Power Mode temporarily reduces power consumption by:
- Disabling automatic downloads and mail fetch
- Reducing visual effects and animations
- Limiting background app activity
- Reducing screen brightness
- Disabling 5G (except for video streaming)

**When to use:**
- Battery below 20%
- Away from charger for extended period
- Need device to last longer

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Method 1: Quick Access from Settings

#### 1. Open Settings

Tap **Settings** on your home screen.
![Settings icon on home screen](/assets/guides/ios_settings_example.png)

#### 2. Go to Battery

Tap **Battery**.
![Battery settings screen](/assets/guides/low-power-mode/battery.png)

#### 3. Enable Low Power Mode

Toggle on **Low Power Mode**.
![Low Power Mode toggle](/assets/guides/low-power-mode/low-power-mode.png)

✅ **Done!** Your device is now in Low Power Mode.

### Method 2: Control Center

#### 1. Open Control Center

- **iPhone with Face ID**: Swipe down from top-right corner
- **iPhone with Home button**: Swipe up from bottom
- **iPad**: Swipe down from top-right corner
![Open Control Center gesture](/assets/guides/low-power-mode/control-center.png)


#### 2. Tap Battery Icon

Tap the **battery icon** to toggle Low Power Mode on/off.
![Battery icon in Control Center](/assets/guides/low-power-mode/battery-icon.png)

### Method 3: Automatic Prompt

When your battery reaches 20% (and again at 10%), iOS will automatically prompt you to enable Low Power Mode.

## What Changes in Low Power Mode?

**Disabled features:**
- Email fetch
- Background app refresh
- Automatic downloads
- Some visual effects
- Auto-Lock (reduced to 30 seconds)

**Reduced performance:**
- Slower processor speed
- Dimmer display
- Limited 5G

**Still works normally:**
- Phone calls and messages
- Internet browsing
- Apps (but may be slower)
- Photos and Camera

## How Long Does It Last?

Low Power Mode automatically turns off when:
- You charge your device to 80% or more
- You manually disable it

## Battery Savings

Typical battery life extension:
- Light use: +2-3 hours
- Moderate use: +1-2 hours
- Heavy use: +30-60 minutes

## Tips for Maximum Battery Life

1. **Enable at 50%** if you know you'll need extra battery later
2. **Combine with airplane mode** when you don't need connectivity
3. **Lower brightness manually** for even more savings
4. **Close unnecessary apps** before enabling

## Works on iPhone and iPad

This feature is available on both iPhone and iPad running iOS 9 or later. The settings are identical on both devices.
