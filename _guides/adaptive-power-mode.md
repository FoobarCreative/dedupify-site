Extend your battery's lifespan by **reducing chemical aging** with iOS's intelligent charging optimization.

## Why This Matters

Optimized Battery Charging learns your daily charging routine and waits to finish charging past 80% until you need to use your device.

**Benefits:**
- Reduces battery wear and aging
- Extends overall battery lifespan
- Maintains battery health longer
- No manual intervention needed

**How it works:**
- iOS learns when you typically charge (e.g., overnight)
- Charges to 80% quickly
- Holds at 80% for most of the charging period
- Finishes charging to 100% just before you typically unplug

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open Settings

Tap **Settings** on your home screen.

![iOS Settings main screen](/assets/guides/adaptive-power-mode/ios_settings_example.png)

### 2. Go to Battery

Tap **Battery**.

### 3. Tap Battery Health & Charging

Tap **Battery Health & Charging** (or **Battery Health** on older iOS versions).

### 4. Enable Optimized Battery Charging

Toggle on **Optimized Battery Charging**.

✅ **Done!** Your iPhone will now learn your charging patterns.

## How Long Does It Take to Learn?

iOS needs about **2 weeks** of regular charging to learn your pattern. During this time:
- Keep a consistent daily routine
- Charge in the same location (iOS uses location data)
- Charge overnight regularly

## When Does It Activate?

Optimized charging only activates when:
- iOS recognizes you're at a familiar location (home, work)
- You're following your regular charging schedule
- The device will be charging for an extended period

## Temporary Override

If you need a full charge immediately:

**iOS 15 and later:**
1. Long-press the battery notification
2. Tap **Charge Now**

**Alternative:**
- Temporarily disable Optimized Battery Charging in Settings

## Additional Battery Health Tips

**For Maximum Battery Lifespan:**
- Avoid extreme temperatures (below 32°F/0°C or above 95°F/35°C)
- Remove thick cases while charging
- Keep your device updated to latest iOS
- Avoid letting battery drop to 0% frequently

## What's the Difference vs Low Power Mode?

**Optimized Battery Charging:**
- Protects battery health long-term
- Learns your habits
- Works during charging
- Always leave it on

**Low Power Mode:**
- Saves battery power short-term
- Reduces performance temporarily
- Use when battery is low
- Turn on/off as needed

## Works on iPhone and iPad

This feature is available on iPhone 8 and later, and iPad Pro (2018 and later) running iOS 13 or iPadOS 13 and later.
