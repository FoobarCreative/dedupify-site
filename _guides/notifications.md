Reduce distractions and save battery by optimizing which apps can send you notifications.

## Why This Matters

Excessive notifications can:
- Interrupt focus and productivity
- Drain battery with screen wake-ups
- Cause unnecessary stress
- Waste time checking unimportant alerts

**Benefits of optimization:**
- Better focus and productivity
- Reduced anxiety and stress
- 5-10% battery savings
- More control over your device

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open Settings

Tap **Settings** on your home screen.
![Settings icon on home screen](/assets/guides/ios_settings_example.png)

### 2. Go to Notifications

Tap **Notifications**.
![Notifications settings](/assets/guides/notifications/notifications.png)

### 3. Review Each App

Scroll through the list and tap each app to configure its notification settings.
![App notifications list](/assets/guides/notifications/app.png)

### 4. Adjust Notification Options

For each app, you can control:
- Allow Notifications (on/off)
- Lock Screen display
- Notification Center display
- Banners
- Badge App Icon
- Sounds
- Critical Alerts
![Notification options](/assets/guides/notifications/notification-options.png)

## Notification Delivery Options

### Immediate Delivery

Notifications appear instantly.

**Best for:**
- Messages and calls
- Calendar events
- Important work apps

### Scheduled Summary

Notifications arrive in a scheduled bundle.

**Configure summary times:**
1. Settings > Notifications > Scheduled Summary
![Scheduled summary](/assets/guides/notifications/scheduled-summary.png)
2. Set up the delivery times (e.g., 9 AM and 6 PM)
![Scheduled summary times](/assets/guides/notifications/choose-apps.png)
![Scheduled summary times](/assets/guides/notifications/add-app.png)
![Scheduled summary times](/assets/guides/notifications/turn-on.png)

**Best for:**
- Social media
- News apps
- Shopping apps
- Non-urgent updates

### Turn Off Completely

**Best for:**
- Apps you rarely use
- Games
- Promotional apps

## Notification Styles

### Banners

Temporary notification that appears at top of screen.

**Options:**
- **Temporary**: Disappears automatically
- **Persistent**: Stays until dismissed

**Recommendation:** Temporary for most apps, Persistent for important apps only.

### Alerts

Requires action to dismiss.

**Recommendation:** Only for critical apps (Calendar, Reminders).

### None

No banner or alert, only appears in Notification Center.

**Recommendation:** For apps you check manually (email, social media).

## Recommended Settings by App Type

### Messaging (Messages, WhatsApp, Slack)
- ✅ Allow Notifications
- ✅ Lock Screen
- ✅ Notification Center
- ✅ Banners (Temporary)
- ✅ Sounds
- ✅ Badge App Icon

### Social Media (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter)
- ⚠️ Allow Notifications (or disable completely)
- ❌ Lock Screen
- ✅ Notification Center
- ❌ Banners
- ❌ Sounds
- ✅ Badge App Icon
- ⚠️ Use Scheduled Summary

### Email
- ✅ Allow Notifications
- ⚠️ Lock Screen (optional)
- ✅ Notification Center
- ❌ Banners (check manually)
- ❌ Sounds
- ✅ Badge App Icon

### News Apps
- ⚠️ Allow Notifications
- ❌ Lock Screen
- ✅ Notification Center
- ❌ Banners
- ❌ Sounds
- ❌ Badge App Icon
- ✅ Use Scheduled Summary

### Games
- ❌ Allow Notifications (disable completely)

### Shopping/Delivery Apps
- ✅ Allow Notifications (for order updates)
- ✅ Lock Screen (for deliveries)
- ✅ Notification Center
- ✅ Banners (Temporary)
- ✅ Sounds
- ✅ Badge App Icon

## Advanced Notification Features

### Focus Modes

Create custom Focus modes to filter notifications:

1. Settings > Focus
2. Choose or create a Focus (Work, Personal, Sleep)
![Choose Focus](/assets/guides/notifications/focus.png)
3. Configure allowed apps and people
![Configure](/assets/guides/notifications/focus-configuration.png)
4. Schedule or enable manually
![Schedule](/assets/guides/notifications/schedule.png)

**Pre-configured modes:**
- Do Not Disturb
- Sleep
- Work
- Personal
- Driving

### Critical Alerts

Allow certain apps to bypass Do Not Disturb and Focus modes.

**Use for:**
- Emergency alerts
- Health apps
- Home security
- Critical work apps

### Notification Previews

Control what shows in notifications:

1. Settings > Notifications > Show Previews
![Show previews](/assets/guides/notifications/show-previews.png)
2. Choose:
   - **Always**: Show message content
   - **When Unlocked**: Only when device unlocked
   - **Never**: Never show content
![Notification previews](/assets/guides/notifications/when-unlocked.png)

**Recommendation:** "When Unlocked" for best balance of privacy and convenience.

## Battery Impact

Notification-related battery usage:
- Screen wake-ups: 5-10% battery per day
- Network fetching: 2-5% battery per day
- Sound/vibration: 1-2% battery per day

**Optimization saves:** 5-10% battery by reducing notifications.

## Notification Grouping

Control how notifications from the same app are grouped:

**Automatic**
- iOS groups intelligently
- Recommended for most apps

**By App**
- All notifications in one stack
- Good for high-volume apps

**Off**
- Each notification separate
- Use for important, low-volume apps

## Quick Notification Management

**From Lock Screen or Notification Center:**
1. Swipe left on a notification
2. Tap **Options** or **...**
![Quick notification options](/assets/guides/notifications/options.png)
3. Choose:
   - Mute for 1 Hour
   - Mute for Today
   - Settings (go to app notification settings)
![Quick notification options](/assets/guides/notifications/options-configuration.png)

## Our Recommendation

**Minimize distractions:**
1. Only enable notifications for 5-10 essential apps
2. Use Scheduled Summary for social media and news
3. Disable Lock Screen notifications for non-urgent apps
4. Turn off sounds for most apps
5. Use Focus modes for work and sleep
6. Check Screen Time weekly to audit notifications

**Result:** Better focus, less stress, longer battery life.

## Works on iPhone and iPad

Notification settings work identically on iPhone and iPad running iOS 7 or later. Scheduled Summary and Focus modes require iOS 15 or later.
