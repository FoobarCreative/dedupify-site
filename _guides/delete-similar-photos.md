Free up **1GB to 15GB** of storage by clearing similar photos and burst shots that pile up around the same moment.

## Why This Matters

Most wasted photo storage is not exact duplicates. It is **similar** photos:
- Burst shots, where one tap captures ten or more frames
- Three or four tries at the same group photo
- Small changes in angle, zoom, or lighting of the same scene
- Retakes of a document, receipt, or whiteboard

These are not exact copies, so the built-in Duplicates album ignores them. They can be the single largest source of hidden clutter in a large library.

## How Similar Photos Differ from Duplicates

- **Duplicates** are the same or near identical. The Photos app can find these in its Duplicates album. See [Delete Duplicate Photos](/guides/delete-duplicate-photos).
- **Similar photos** are different shots of one moment. No built-in album groups these, so you clear them by hand or with a dedicated cleaner.

## Method 1: Review and Delete by Hand

### 1. Open the Photos App

Tap **Photos**, then the **Library** tab. Use **Days** or **All Photos** view to see sets together.

### 2. Thin Out Each Set

When you see several shots of one moment, tap **Select**, keep the best, and select the rest.

### 3. Delete the Extras

Tap the trash icon to remove the selected photos.

✅ **Done!** The extra shots move to Recently Deleted.

## Method 2: Clear Bursts

Bursts are stored as one stack, but every frame still uses space.

### 1. Open a Burst

In Photos, bursts show a stacked icon and the label **Burst**.

### 2. Select the Keepers

Tap **Select** at the bottom, then mark the frames you want.

### 3. Keep Only Favorites

Tap **Done** and choose **Keep Only Favorites**. The frames you did not pick move to Recently Deleted.

## Method 3: Group and Clear Similar Photos Automatically

Reviewing a large library by hand takes a long time, and it is easy to miss sets. [Dedupify](/) scans your library, groups visually similar photos and bursts together, highlights the best shot in each group, and lets you clear the rest with a swipe. It handles the similar photos the Duplicates album leaves behind.

This is the fastest option when you have thousands of photos and years of burst shots.

## Reclaim the Space

Deleted photos sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use storage. To free the space now, open **Albums → Recently Deleted**, tap **Select**, then **Delete All**.

## How Much Space Will I Save?

Savings depend on how often you shoot bursts and retakes:
- Light shooters: 1GB to 3GB
- Regular users: 3GB to 8GB
- Heavy or burst heavy libraries: 8GB to 15GB or more

To check your photo storage: **Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Photos**.

## Important Notes

⚠️ **Keep one of each moment.** Review a set before you delete so you do not lose the only good shot.

💡 **Tip:** Clear exact copies first with the [Delete Duplicate Photos](/guides/delete-duplicate-photos) guide, then clear the similar shots that remain.

## Works on iPhone and iPad

This guide works on both iPhone and iPad from iOS 13. The steps use the standard Photos app.
