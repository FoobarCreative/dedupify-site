Free up **1-10GB instantly** by permanently deleting photos and videos from your Recently Deleted album.

## Why This Matters

When you delete photos or videos on your iPhone or iPad, they aren't immediately removed. Instead, they sit in the **Recently Deleted** album for 30 days, still taking up storage space.

**The Problem:**
- Deleted photos continue using storage for up to 30 days
- Can accumulate several gigabytes over time
- Most users forget to empty this album

**The Solution:**
Empty the Recently Deleted album to reclaim storage immediately.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Open the Photos App

Tap the **Photos** icon on your home screen.
![Photos app on home screen](/assets/guides/clear-recently-deleted/photos.png)

### 2. Scroll to Recently Deleted

Scroll down to the **Utilities** section and tap **Recently Deleted**.
![Recently Deleted in Utilities](/assets/guides/clear-recently-deleted/recently-deleted.png)

### 3. Empty the Album

Tap **Select** in the top right, then tap **Delete All** in the bottom left.
![Delete All in Recently Deleted](/assets/guides/clear-recently-deleted/select.png)
![Delete All in Recently Deleted](/assets/guides/clear-recently-deleted/delete-all.png)

### 4. Confirm Deletion

Tap **Delete From All Devices** to permanently remove them.
![Confirm delete photos popup](/assets/guides/clear-recently-deleted/delete-from-all-devices.png)

✅ **Done!** Storage is freed up immediately.

## How Much Space Will I Save?

The Recently Deleted album shows how much space it's using at the top. Common savings:
- Light users: 500MB - 2GB
- Regular users: 2GB - 5GB
- Heavy users: 5GB - 10GB+

## Important Notes

⚠️ **This action cannot be undone.** Photos are permanently deleted.

💡 **Tip:** Check this album monthly as part of device maintenance.

## What Gets Deleted

The Recently Deleted album contains:
- Photos you deleted in the last 30 days
- Videos you deleted in the last 30 days
- Screenshots and screen recordings you deleted

Items are automatically deleted after 30 days, but you can reclaim space immediately by emptying the album manually.

## Works on iPhone and iPad

This guide applies to both iPhone and iPad running iOS 8 or later. The Photos app interface is identical on both devices.
