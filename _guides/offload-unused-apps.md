Save **5-20GB of storage** by offloading apps you rarely use while keeping all your data safe.

## Why This Matters

Apps can be surprisingly large:
- Games: 1-5GB each
- Social media apps: 500MB - 2GB
- Navigation apps: 500MB - 1GB

**Offloading vs Deleting:**
- **Offloading:** Removes the app but keeps all data and settings
- **Deleting:** Removes everything (app + data)

When you reinstall an offloaded app, everything returns exactly as you left it.

## Automatic Offloading (Recommended)

Let iOS automatically offload apps you don't use:

### 1. Open Settings

Tap **Settings** on your home screen.

### 2. Go to App Store

Scroll down and tap **App Store**.

### 3. Enable Offload Unused Apps

Toggle on **Offload Unused Apps**.

✅ **Done!** iOS will automatically offload apps when storage is low.

## Manual Offloading

Offload specific apps manually:

### 1. Open Settings

Tap **Settings** on your home screen.

### 2. Go to General

Tap **General**.

### 3. Tap iPhone Storage

Tap **iPhone Storage** (or **iPad Storage** on iPad).

### 4. Select an App

Scroll through your apps and tap one to see details.

### 5. Offload the App

Tap **Offload App** and confirm.

## Which Apps Should You Offload?

Good candidates for offloading:
- ✅ Games you finished but might replay
- ✅ Seasonal apps (tax software, holiday planners)
- ✅ Apps you use occasionally
- ✅ Large apps with cloud-synced data

**Don't offload:**
- ❌ Apps you use daily
- ❌ Apps with local-only data you need
- ❌ System apps

## How to Reinstall

When you tap an offloaded app's icon, it automatically reinstalls from the App Store with all your data intact.

## How Much Space Will I Save?

Check iPhone Storage (Settings → General → iPhone Storage) to see:
- How large each app is
- When you last used it
- Recommendations for apps to offload

Typical savings: **5-20GB** depending on which apps you offload.

## Works on iPhone and iPad

This feature works identically on both iPhone and iPad running iOS 11 or later.
